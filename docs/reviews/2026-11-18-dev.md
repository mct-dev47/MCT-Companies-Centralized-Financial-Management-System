## Summary
This change links `LoanRepayment` records to their borrower `Expense` and lender `BankTransaction` rows (migration `0074`, startup backfill, and atomic cascading edits/deletes across expenses, bills, loans, and bank ledgers), adds `bank_account_id` editing support to expenses, and wires `BILLS_UPDATED` WebSocket refreshes on the frontend. Overall the transactional cascade and no-op `Verified` preservation design are well structured, with pre-validation blocking bad edits before commit. However, shipping is blocked by an immediate `NameError` in `_company_loan_item` that breaks `/api/v1/loans/company` and `/api/v1/loans/company/export`, alongside major sync bugs on legacy loan repayments, expense-to-repayment matching, `amount_paid`-only edits, and triplicated/incomplete tests.

## Blockers
### 🔴 Undefined `principal` crashes `_company_loan_item`, `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/loans.py:194-195`
**Problem**: `_company_loan_item` computes `total_payable = _loan_total_payable(loan)` (line `167`) without defining a local `principal` variable, then passes `principal_amount=principal` (line `194`) and `amount=principal` (line `195`) when constructing `CompanyLoanOut`.
**Why it matters**: `_group_company_loans` (`loans.py:217`) invokes `_company_loan_item` for every loan returned by `GET /api/v1/loans/company` (`loans.py:297`) and `GET /api/v1/loans/company/export` (`loans.py:364`). Both endpoints will fail with `NameError: name 'principal' is not defined` (HTTP 500) whenever any disbursed loan exists.
**Suggested fix**: Define `principal = Decimal(str(loan.principal_amount or 0))` inside `_company_loan_item` before instantiating `CompanyLoanOut`.

## Major
### 🟠 Changing repayment amount on an unlinked legacy repayment breaks lender bank adoption, `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/loans.py:2284-2303`
**Problem**: Both `sync_loan_repayment_from_expense` (`loans.py:2284`, `:2296-2303`) and `update_loan_repayment` (`loans.py:2388`, `:2410-2417`) overwrite `rep.amount = new_amt` before calling `_sync_lender_bank_for_repayment`. When no `BankTransaction` yet carries `source_loan_repayment_id`, `_sync_lender_bank_for_repayment` calls `_find_legacy_lender_deposit` (`:2150-2151`), passing `match_date=legacy_pay_date` for date changes while `_find_legacy_lender_deposit` still filters `BankTransaction.deposit == repayment.amount` (`:2108-2109`) using the new amount. In addition, `_assert_lender_bank_unlocked` (`:2113-2127`) only queries `source_loan_repayment_id == repayment_id`, so a `Verified` unlinked legacy lender deposit is not caught during `prepare_loan_repayment_edit` upfront validation (it only fails later inside `_sync_lender_bank_for_repayment`).
**Why it matters**: Editing the amount of any repayment whose lender deposit has not yet been linked with `source_loan_repayment_id` silently fails to match and update that deposit, leaving the lender's bank ledger and balance out of sync with the borrower expense and repayment record.
**Suggested fix**: Capture `prior_amount = rep.amount` before mutating `rep.amount` and pass `legacy_amount` through `_sync_lender_bank_for_repayment` into `_find_legacy_lender_deposit`, and also check `_find_legacy_lender_deposit` as a fallback inside `_assert_lender_bank_unlocked`.

### 🟠 `_find_repayment_for_expense` fallback can claim an already-linked `LoanRepayment`, `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/loans.py:2048-2061`
**Problem**: When `LoanRepayment.expense_id == expense.expense_id` returns no row, `_find_repayment_for_expense` matches `LoanRepayment` rows by `(Loan.borrower, payment_date, amount)` ordered by `loan_repayment_id.asc()` without filtering `LoanRepayment.expense_id.is_(None)` or checking lender wording (`(to {l_name})`) as `backfill_loan_repayment_links` (`:2564-2578`) does. Calling code in `prepare_loan_repayment_edit` (`:2228-2230`) and `sync_loan_repayment_from_expense` (`:2256`) then overwrites `rep.expense_id = expense.expense_id`, and `delete_loan_repayment_from_expense` (`:2317-2324`) deletes whichever row matched.
**Why it matters**: If a borrower has two equal-amount repayments on the same day (for example, two loans or an existing linked repayment plus an unlinked expense/repayment), editing or deleting the unlinked expense can match the lowest-ID repayment, steal its `expense_id`, or delete the wrong repayment and its lender deposit.
**Suggested fix**: Restrict the fallback query in `_find_repayment_for_expense` with `LoanRepayment.expense_id.is_(None)` and apply the same `(to {l_name})` disambiguation used in `backfill_loan_repayment_links`.

### 🟠 `amount_paid`-only expense updates diverge from synced bank, bill, and loan amounts, `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/expenses.py:1164-1178`
**Problem**: In `update_expense`, sending `amount_paid` without `amount`/`withdrawal` and without `particulars`/`description` hits the `elif expense_in.amount_paid is not None` branch (`:1171-1173`), which sets `effective_paid_amount` and writes `expense.amount_paid = expense_in.amount_paid` (`:1261-1262`). Pre-validation (`prepare_loan_repayment_edit` and `BillsService.prepare_payment_edit_from_expense`, `:1187-1188`) validates that `effective_paid_amount`, but `reconcile_expense_bank` (`:78`), `BillsService.sync_payment_from_expense` (`bills_service.py:786`), and `sync_loan_repayment_from_expense` (`loans.py:2261`) ignore `expense.amount_paid` and recompute paid amount via `compute_paid_amount_from_particulars(expense.description, expense.amount)`.
**Why it matters**: An `amount_paid`-only `PUT`/`PATCH` leaves `Expense.amount_paid` out of sync with the bank disbursement, `BillPayment.amount`, and `LoanRepayment.amount`, and bypasses the pre-mutation `prepare_*` validation checks against what actually gets written downstream.
**Suggested fix**: Derive `effective_paid_amount` consistently from `compute_paid_amount_from_particulars(base_desc, base_amt)` (rejecting or ignoring conflicting standalone `amount_paid` overrides), or pass the validated paid amount explicitly to bank, bill, and loan sync helpers.

### 🟠 Duplicate shadowed tests and missing branch/error coverage, `backend/tests/test_review_fixes_bank_sync.py:283-1092`
**Problem**: Four module-level test functions in `backend/tests/test_review_fixes_bank_sync.py` are defined three times each (`test_http_delete_expense_cascades_to_bills_and_loans_atomically` at lines `283`, `382`, `853`; `test_http_verified_lender_deposit_locks_repayment_edits_and_deletes_409` at lines `481`, `623`, `952`; `test_http_repayment_payment_method_omitted_preserves_petty_cash_and_switches_cleanly` at lines `530`, `672`, `1001`; `test_patch_linked_entry_preserves_verified_on_noop_and_recompute_loan_skips_rest_when_shared_db` at lines `582`, `724`, `1053`), so Python overwrites the earlier definitions and pytest silently skips 8 of the 12 definitions (collecting 15 tests instead of 23). In addition, several new/changed branching paths lack tests:

- `PUT /expenses/{id}` switching `bank_account_id` or toggling `payment_method` between Bank and Petty Cash while linked to a `BillPayment` or `LoanRepayment` (`expenses.py:1197-1216`, `:1281-1283`), and invalid `bank_account_id` rejection on `PUT /expenses/{id}`.

**Why it matters**: With `TESTS = configured`, duplicated definitions create false confidence while critical error, overpay, and company-loan endpoints execute completely unexercised in CI.
**Suggested fix**: Remove the triplicated earlier definitions (or merge unique assertions under distinct test names), omit `"amount"` in the particulars-only overpay test, and add HTTP/migration tests covering `/loans/company`, bank-account & Petty Cash switches via `PUT /expenses/{id}`, `amount_paid`-only updates, and `0073`.

### 🟡 `recompute_loan_repayments_and_status` strips `"Overdue"` status on edit/delete, `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/loans.py:2014-2021`
**Problem**: `recompute_loan_repayments_and_status` always assigns `loan.status` to one of `"Disbursed"`, `"Paid"`, or `"Partially Paid"`, ignoring whether `loan.due_date` is already in the past (unlike `_company_loan_item` at `:179-180`).
**Why it matters**: Editing or deleting a partial repayment on an overdue loan silently downgrades its stored `Loan.status` from `"Overdue"` to `"Partially Paid"` or `"Disbursed"`.
**Suggested fix**: Preserve/compute `"Overdue"` when `remaining > Decimal("0.00")` and `loan.due_date` is past due.

### 🟡 `_sync_lender_bank_for_repayment` duplicates `BankService.patch_linked_entry` logic, `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/loans.py:2130-2194`
**Problem**: `_sync_lender_bank_for_repayment` reimplements duplicate cleanup, the field-change comparison that keeps `Verified` status on no-ops, and `_sync_balance` flushing separately from `BankService.patch_linked_entry` (`bank_service.py:698-794`), and does not update `company_name` or `particulars` on the kept lender row.
**Why it matters**: Two parallel implementations of linked-bank patching increase drift risk (as seen with `legacy_amount` and `company_name` handling) when audit-lock or balance rules change.
**Suggested fix**: Extend `BankService.patch_linked_entry` (or a shared helper on `BankService`) to handle `source_loan_repayment_id` deposits cleanly.
