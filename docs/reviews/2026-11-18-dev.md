## Blockers
### 🔴 Standalone Sales, Expenses, and Bank rows locked out from editing and deleting, `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/transactions.py:220`
**Problem**: `list_transactions` sets `sourceModule=src_mod or "Sales"` on every standalone Sale (`backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/transactions.py:220`), `sourceModule=src_mod or "Expense"` on every standalone Expense (`line 304`), and `sourceModule="Bank"` on every manual `BankTransaction` (`line 619`). `frontend/src/services/transactionService.ts:101` forwards `sourceModule` verbatim into UI rows, whereas `canEditTx` (`frontend/src/pages/TransactionPage.tsx:1674`), `canShowDeleteOption` (`TransactionPage.tsx:1682`), and the newly added guard in `isEditableType` (`frontend/src/components/dashboard/AuditTransactionModal.tsx:636`) all require `!sourceModule`. Together with preserving `sourceModule` in `preserveLedgerFields` (`AuditTransactionModal.tsx:563`) and `handleSaveRemarks` (`TransactionPage.tsx:1942, 1958`), every standalone Sale, Expense, and manual Bank row on `/transactions` now evaluates `!sourceModule` to `false`. (By contrast, `salesService.ts:172` and `expenseService.ts:161` receive `source_module: None` for standalone entries.)
**Why it matters**: Finance and Supervisor users on `/transactions` cannot edit any unaudited standalone Sale or Expense (either from the row action menu or inside `AuditTransactionModal`), and cannot delete any unaudited standalone Sale, Expense, or manual Bank entry, even though the backend cascade delete endpoint explicitly supports them.
**Suggested fix**: In `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/transactions.py:220, 304, 619`, keep `sourceModule` as `None` unless the entry originates from an external module (`src_mod` such as `"Tenants"`, `"Bills"`, `"Lending"`, `"Receivables"`, or `"Petty Cash"`), or have the frontend guards check explicitly for external origin modules instead of `!sourceModule`.

## Minor
### 🟡 `isEditableType` includes `isLoan` without a loan editor route, `frontend/src/components/dashboard/AuditTransactionModal.tsx:640`
**Problem**: `isEditableType` at `frontend/src/components/dashboard/AuditTransactionModal.tsx:635-643` still includes `isLoan`, while `handleEditInSheet` (`lines 646 to 662`) only branches on `isExpense` (`/expense/add`) versus `/sales/add`. Because `transactions.py` leaves `sourceModule` unset on `Loan` rows, clicking Edit on an unaudited `Loan` row in `AuditTransactionModal` navigates to `/sales/add` with `editSale: null`.
**Why it matters**: Non admin/auditor writers opening an unaudited disbursed loan in `AuditTransactionModal` are offered an Edit button that opens the Sales sheet instead of a loan editor.
**Suggested fix**: Remove `isLoan` from `isEditableType` at `frontend/src/components/dashboard/AuditTransactionModal.tsx:640`.

### 🟡 Missing regression tests for new Section 3, Section 5, and `sourceModule` branches, `backend/tests/test_transactions_endpoint.py:1038`
**Problem**: The newest additions in `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/transactions.py` lack explicit assertions in `backend/tests/test_transactions_endpoint.py`: (a) `All Companies` cross company petty cash emission returning both `petty_{id}` and `petty_{id}:recipient` (`isMirrorRow=True`, `withdrawal=0.0`) at `lines 419 to 426`, (b) petty cash funded by a shared bank co owned via `BankAccount.company_names` staying single row (`co_owner_cids_by_bank`, `lines 338 to 345, 416`), (c) Section 5 loan `disb_bank_by_loan` matching `BankTransaction.cheque_no == loan_ref_no` and own first versus shared fallback ordering (`lines 647 to 708`), and (d) standalone versus external `sourceModule` values on Sales, Expenses, and manual Bank rows (`lines 220, 304, 619`).
**Why it matters**: Under `TESTS = configured`, untested branching logic in the ledger projection risks silent regressions in UI gating and cross company presentation.
**Suggested fix**: Add focused cases in `backend/tests/test_transactions_endpoint.py` covering `All Companies` `:recipient` mirror rows, co owned shared bank petty cash, `cheque_no` loan disbursement bank resolution, and `sourceModule` output contracts.
