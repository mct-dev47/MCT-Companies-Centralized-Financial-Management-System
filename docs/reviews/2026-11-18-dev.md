## Blockers
### 🔴 `NameError` crash and split brain delete on `exp_` transactions, `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/transactions.py:795`
**Problem**: In `delete_transaction`, the `exp_` branch (`transactions.py:788-800`) deletes the remote row from Supabase at lines 788 to 791 and then calls `reconcile_expense_bank(db, expense_id=eid, force_zero=True)` at line 795. However, `reconcile_expense_bank` (defined in `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/expenses.py:62`) is never imported anywhere in `transactions.py:1-36`. Furthermore, `exp_` (`773-778`) and `sale_` (`830-835`) only inspect local SQLite rows instead of checking Supabase first like `_current_expense_row` (`expenses.py:156-163`) and `delete_sale` (`sales.py:1758-1790`), `purge_linked_entries` at lines 800 and 852 runs after `db.commit()` without guarding errors, and `loan_rep_` IDs (`902`) still fail `.isdigit()` and return 404.
**Why it matters**: Any request to `DELETE /api/v1/transactions/exp_{id}` for an unlocked expense not linked to `BillPayment` or `Lending` deletes the Supabase expense record first and immediately crashes with HTTP 500 `NameError`, leaving the local SQLite `Expense` and linked `BankTransaction` intact in a split brain state. (`TransactionPage.tsx:1683-1686` currently routes expense and sale deletes to `/expenses/{id}` and `/sales/{id}`, hiding this bug during UI clicks.)
**Suggested fix**: Remove the duplicated delete code in `transactions.py` and delegate `exp_` and `sale_` deletes to shared service helpers or the canonical `expenses.delete_expense` and `sales.delete_sale` logic, ensuring `reconcile_expense_bank` is imported or encapsulated, Supabase `Verified` status is checked before any mutation, and linked bank purge failures are handled cleanly.

### 🔴 Missing loan status filter emits unapproved, undisbursed, and rejected loans as Paid cash movements, `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/transactions.py:493`
**Problem**: Section 5 of `list_transactions` (`transactions.py:493-504`) queries every `Loan` matching the company and date scope without filtering `Loan.status` against `DISBURSED_LOAN_STATUSES` (`["disbursed", "paid", "partially paid", "overdue"]` in `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/loans.py:1214` and Spec 0046 `docs/specs/0046-loan-disbursement-step.md:37`). All loan rows are returned with `status="Paid"`, `awaitingAudit=False`, `deposit=principal` on the borrower row (`535-545`), and `withdrawal=principal` on the lender row (`563-575`).
**Why it matters**: Every `Pending Finance Endorsement`, `Pending Audit Approval`, `Approved` (not yet disbursed), `Rejected by Finance`, and `Disapproved by Audit` loan passes the `!t.awaitingAudit` check in `frontend/src/pages/TransactionPage.tsx:834` and inflates cash deposits, withdrawals, and Bank Ledger running balances even when no cash left or entered any bank.
**Suggested fix**: Filter Section 5 `Loan` queries to `DISBURSED_LOAN_STATUSES` (case insensitive), matching `loans.py` and Spec 0046, so only loans whose funds were disbursed appear in `TransactionPage`.

## Major
### 🟠 Petty cash section double counts disbursements, marks unsettled claims Paid, and misclassifies transfer states, `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/transactions.py:294`
**Problem**: Section 3 (`transactions.py:294-371`) diverges from `PettyCashService.list_movements` (`backend/app/services/petty_cash_service.py:895-926`) in four ways:
1. It includes `request_type == "disbursement"` rows (`PC-DISB-` records already create a paired `Expense` in Section 2, double counting them) and zero/negative amount reimbursements;
2. It marks `Approved` or `Settled` reimbursements (`status != "reimbursed"`) and petty cash funded payouts (`payment_method_option == "petty_cash"`) as `status="Paid"`, `paidAmount=amt`, `withdrawal=amt if p.bank_account_id else 0.0`, even though `Approved` reimbursements have not yet moved bank cash and `petty_cash` payouts already create an `Expense`;
3. `"Transfer In Transit"` (lacking `"pending"`) falls into `else:` (`status="Paid"`, `auditStatus="Verified"`, `withdrawal=amt`), while `"Return Pending"` (containing `"pending"`) falls into `elif "pending":` (`status="Pending"`, `awaitingAudit=True`, `withdrawal=0.0`) despite bank funds already having moved and Audit having approved (`petty_cash_service.py:268-275, 2290-2310`);
4. Filtering `transactions.py` by `company_id` or `company_name` checks only `PettyCashTopUp.company_id`, dropping cross company petty cash withdrawals funded by that company's bank (`petty_cash_service.py:925`). Meanwhile, `AuditTransactionModal.tsx:380-382` checks `pc_` rather than `petty_` for timeline lookup.
**Why it matters**: Ledger totals, Petty Cash rows, and Bank Ledger balances diverge between `PettyCashPage` and `TransactionPage` whenever disbursements, reimbursements, or intercompany transfers are present.
**Suggested fix**: Align Section 3 with `PettyCashService.list_movements` rules (`disbursement` exclusion, `reimbursed` status requirement for cash payout movements, accurate status mapping for `Transfer In Transit` and `Return Pending`, and `funding_company_id` scoping), and match `petty_` in `AuditTransactionModal.tsx`.

### 🟠 Intercompany bank transfers leak past deduplication, shared banks misattribute null company rows, and queries remain unbounded, `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/transactions.py:383`
**Problem**: `PettyCashService` intercompany transfer and repayment helpers (`backend/app/services/petty_cash_service.py:2194-2198, 2257-2261, 2471-2485`) call `BankService.withdraw` and `BankService.deposit` without setting `entry_source` (`bank_service.py:486, 522`), using `category="Bank Transfer"` and particulars beginning with `"Intercompany Transfer ..."` or `"Intercompany Repayment ..."`. When `entry_source` is `NULL`, `transactions.py:387-392` only filters out `loan%`, `%petty cash%`, `"Loan Repayment"`, and `"Loan"`, allowing intercompany transfers into Section 4 `Bank`. In addition, lines `407-410` and `418-420` still map `company_name IS NULL` entries on shared accounts to `BankAccount.company_name`, and `list_transactions` (`79, 134, 225, 306, 414, 504, 634`) leaves `limit=None` by default so `frontend/src/services/transactionService.ts:24-50` loads full tables via `.all()` before sorting and slicing in Python.
**Why it matters**: Intercompany bank movements can appear twice, unlabelled shared account entries can leak or be attributed to the primary company, and default calls do unbounded full table reads across 6 tables.
**Suggested fix**: Mark automated `BankService.deposit` and `withdraw` calls with `entry_source='system'` (and exclude `"Intercompany %"` in the null fallback), avoid inheriting `BankAccount.company_name` on shared accounts when `BankTransaction.company_name` is missing, and use bounded pagination defaults.

### 🟠 Editing `sale_` rows from Transactions fails receivable lookup and creates duplicate receivables, `frontend/src/pages/AddTransactionSheetPage.tsx:2097`

**Suggested fix**: Strip the `"sale_"` prefix using `stripSalePrefix` (or regex replacement) wherever `AddTransactionSheetPage.tsx` parses `editItem.id` into a numeric sale ID.

### 🟠 Non atomic `BillsService` deletion and swallowed Supabase errors, `backend/app/services/bills_service.py:652`
**Problem**: In `BillsService._remove_funding_expense` (`bills_service.py:636-670`), `reconcile_expense_bank` invokes `BankService.sync_expense_disbursement` (`backend/app/services/bank_service.py:777`, which calls `self.db.commit()`) and `BankService.purge_linked_entries` (`bank_service.py:880`, which also calls `self.db.commit()`) inside each payment iteration before `delete_bill` (`439-440`) or `delete_payment` (`679-680`) commits.
